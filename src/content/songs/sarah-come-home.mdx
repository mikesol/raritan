Days before graduation from college in 2005, I wrote this song for a friend of mine.

I imagine an Agbadza drum circle with guitars interspersed, one of whom is singing. Other players sing along from time to time — the chorus is built for it, a call that wants a response.

I must have played this song once a month for 20+ years. Singing the chorus makes me feel alive.
