Sketches for this song started back in 2006 with a tune called _Il n'y a aucun besoin_ that I wrote about a panda. In 2019, I cannibalized the chorus to create *Lover Cover Me*, a song about how love evolves over time.

The model for this song is Eric Clapton, who I was fortunate enough to see live in New York City in the late 90s. That concert still moves me today. Clapton is a great teacher of any aspiring songwriter, and I hope you hear what I've learned from him in this song.
