Some phrases, uttered by some people, lodge themselves in your head in a way that turns musical over time. Such is the case with _C'est quoi ce bordel_, which I must have heard 3-4 times a day over a multi-year period. The bordel in question changed frequently, but the prosody rarely did.

<audio controls src="/audio/cest-quoi-ce-bordel/cest-quoi-ce-bordel.mp3" style="width:100%;margin:1.5rem 0"></audio>

I must have written the song in 2010-2012, although it's only around 2022 that I figured out what I wanted to do with the bridge, and only in 2026 that I composed the 8-part canon in the outro.
